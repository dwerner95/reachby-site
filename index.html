How the free pilot works

Three simple steps. One comparison sent back to you.

  1. 01

    Send your journey

    Share broad UK and German localities, an arrival time, party size and the trade-offs that matter to you.

  2. 02

    We prepare the comparison

    Pilot requests are researched manually. We check sensible route families and record source timing, likely total, changes and important unknowns.

  3. 03

    You receive the result

    We send a comparison with the complete trade-off explained in plain English. You decide what to do next.

What the report examines

The bits ordinary search tabs leave you to piece together.

Straight answers

Questions about the pilot

Is ReachBy a booking site?

No. ReachBy does not sell, issue, change or refund transport tickets. You decide whether to buy directly from a seller.

Are the results automatic or live?

No. The pilot is researched manually. Every report states when sources were checked and where information is incomplete.

Does the pilot cost anything?

No. The current invite-only pilot is free. We may ask what the result would have been worth, but we will not take payment.

What information should I send?

Start with your origin and destination locality, arrival time, party size and priority. Do not include an exact address, payment detail, booking reference, passport detail or health/accessibility information.
