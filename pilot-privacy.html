Pilot privacy note

This note explains the deliberately small amount of information used in ReachBy's free, human-researched pilot. It applies to the pilot only, not to a future automated or paid product.

Who is responsible

The pilot is operated by Dominik Werner under the ReachBy working name. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to dominik@reachby.app.

What this website does

The website has no form, account, analytics, advertising tracker or non-essential cookie. It does not store journey details in your browser. If you select an email link, your own email service handles the message you choose to send.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and ordinary technical request logs to deliver and secure the site. GitHub controls the retention of those host-side logs; ReachBy does not receive or use them for analytics or marketing.

Information used for a pilot journey

If you choose to take part, ReachBy asks only for the information needed to research the journey: contact via the existing conversation, broad origin and destination localities, travel window, party and baggage needs, relevant preferences, and your feedback.

Please do not send exact home addresses, exact coordinates, payment details, identity documents or booking references. If a more precise endpoint is genuinely necessary, the operator will explain why before recording it.

Use, access and deletion

Pilot information is used only to respond, manually research the requested journey, prepare the comparison, collect feedback and improve the method. Access is limited to Dominik Werner. A separate reviewer may see a case only when one is appointed and the review is necessary.

The current pilot rule is to delete the private working record no later than 30 days after the report and feedback, or sooner on request. A reduced benchmark candidate is not counted or committed until a human checks that it does not contain participant identity, contact, exact location or private free text.

Your choices

Taking part is voluntary. You can decide not to answer an optional question, stop the pilot, ask what information is held, correct it, or ask for deletion by emailing the operator. ReachBy will explain if any requested journey cannot be researched safely with the information you choose to provide.

Simple version

No tracking. No website form. Minimum journey details. One operator. Short deletion period.
